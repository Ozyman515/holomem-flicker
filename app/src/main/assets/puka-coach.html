Set up the board

Pick a piece, then tap a square. Use the + and − buttons for the benches.

How the pieces move

Dots on each piece show the squares it can step to. A Turtle that walks onto the far-right column turns gold. A knocked-off holomem joins the bench of whoever knocked it off, and a Gold Turtle goes back to a plain Turtle.

Game plan

  • Two ways to win: knock off their Leader, or walk your Leader onto their back column (far right) and survive their next turn (a Try).
  • The bench decides games. Every piece you knock off can be dropped onto any empty square later. Trade only when you get something back, and a Dolphin or Whale on the bench is worth more than one on the board.
  • Watch for the Try. If their Leader can reach your back column on a square you can't hit next turn, stop it now, even at the cost of a piece.
  • Turtles are cheap until they turn gold. A Gold Turtle moves almost like a Leader. Drop a Turtle one column from their back column to threaten promotion.
  • Opening: with perfect play the side that moves second wins, so the coach may say you're behind at the start. The computer opponents don't play perfectly, so follow its moves and wait for their mistake.
  • Normal mode gives you 3 undos, so on a stage you can try the coach's line, and back up if it surprises you.
